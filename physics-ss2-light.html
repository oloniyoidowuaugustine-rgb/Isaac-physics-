Topic: Rectilinear Propagation of Light

1. Definition

Rectilinear propagation of light means that **light travels in straight lines in a homogeneous medium**.

2. Observation

  • Light casts **sharp shadows** behind opaque objects.
  • Pinhole experiments demonstrate straight-line travel.
  • Laser beams illustrate rectilinear propagation.

3. Applications

  • Formation of shadows
  • Pinhole cameras
  • Design of optical instruments

4. Notes

  • Light changes direction only when passing into another medium (refraction) or reflecting off a surface.
  • Shadow size and sharpness depend on distance between object and screen.
Example: Place a pencil in a beam of sunlight through a small hole; a sharp shadow appears on the screen behind, showing straight-line travel.
