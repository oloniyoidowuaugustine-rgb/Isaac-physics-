Topic: Effects of State Change

1. Definition

Effects of state change refer to the **physical changes that occur when a substance changes from one state to another** (solid, liquid, gas).

2. Types of State Changes

  • Melting: Solid → Liquid
  • Freezing: Liquid → Solid
  • Evaporation: Liquid → Gas
  • Condensation: Gas → Liquid
  • Sublimation: Solid → Gas directly

3. Effects on Substances

  • Change in volume (e.g., water expands when freezing)
  • Change in density (solid ice less dense than liquid water)
  • Change in energy: energy absorbed or released (latent heat)
  • No change in temperature during the phase change

4. Examples

Example 1: Ice melts → absorbs heat → becomes water → no temperature change until fully melted.
Example 2: Water boils → absorbs heat → becomes steam → temperature remains 100°C until all water vaporizes.

5. Notes

  • Phase changes involve energy transfer without temperature change.
  • Density and volume changes explain floating of ice, inflation of hot air balloons, etc.
  • Understanding state change is key in heating/cooling applications.
