Topic: Physics in Technology & Energy Systems

1. Physics in Technology

Physics principles are applied in technology to improve efficiency, safety, and communication.

  • Electronics → computers, smartphones
  • Optics → cameras, microscopes
  • Energy → generators, turbines
  • Transport → cars, trains, airplanes
  • Medical → X-rays, MRI, ECG

2. Energy Systems

Energy systems convert one form of energy into another to do work efficiently.

  • Mechanical energy systems: Engines, turbines, pumps
  • Electrical energy systems: Generators, transformers, power grids
  • Renewable energy systems: Solar panels, wind turbines, hydroelectric plants

3. Efficiency

Efficiency of an energy system is the ratio of useful energy output to total energy input.

Efficiency = (Useful energy output / Total energy input) × 100%

4. Notes

  • Physics helps design safer, faster, and more efficient machines.
  • Renewable energy systems reduce environmental pollution.
  • Energy conservation is critical for sustainable development.
