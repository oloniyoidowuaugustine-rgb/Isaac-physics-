Meaning of physics

meaning of physics: physics is a branch of science that deals with the study of matter

meaning of matter: Matter is anything that have weight and occupy space. matter exist in three state, namely:liquid, solid and gas

meaning of atom: Atom is the smallest indivisible particles of an element, which can take place in chemical reaction

meaning of molecules: Molecules is the smallest particle of a substance, in which when separated can still retain their physical properties.

kinetic theory of matter: According to the kinetic theory of matte,r, every substance consist of very large number of very small particles called molecules

The kinetic theory of matter is based on the following:

  • matter is made up of molecules
  • molecules are in rapid motion
  • molecules experience force of attraction i.e, they are attracted to one another

    • General properties of matter

  • Matter is made up of tiny particles, molecules or atom
  • matter occupies space
  • Matter possesses inertial,mass or weight.
  • matter exerts pressure

    • Molecular force

  • Adhesive force: Adhesive Force refers to the force which makes molecules of different substance to attract. Adhesive force occurs on water in a glass cup

  • Cohesive force: cohesive force refers to the force which makes molecules of the same substance attract.
    • cohesive force occurs in the case of mercury put in glass

    Capillarity

    capillarity is the tendency of a liquid to move up and down through a narrow tube

    Mass and weight

  • mass is defined as the quantity of a matter in a body. It is measured in kilogram

  • weight is the gravitational pull of a body

    • The relationship between the mass and weight

    weight=mass×gravity
