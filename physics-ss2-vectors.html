Topic: Vector and Vector Resolution

1. Vector

A vector is a quantity that has both **magnitude and direction**. Examples: displacement, velocity, force.

2. Scalar

A scalar is a quantity that has **magnitude only**, no direction. Examples: distance, speed, mass.

3. Vector Resolution

Vector resolution is the process of breaking a vector into **two or more components**, usually along perpendicular axes (x and y).

Vx = V × cosθ
Vy = V × sinθ

Where:
V = magnitude of vector
θ = angle with the horizontal axis
Vx = horizontal component, Vy = vertical component

4. Resultant of Vectors

The resultant vector R can be found using Pythagoras’ theorem when components are perpendicular:

R = √(Vx² + Vy²)
Example: A force of 10 N acts at 30° to the horizontal. Horizontal component: Vx = 10 × cos30° ≈ 8.66 N Vertical component: Vy = 10 × sin30° = 5 N
Example 2: A displacement vector has components 3 m east and 4 m north. Resultant displacement: R = √(3² + 4²) = 5 m

5. Notes

  • Vector resolution is essential for analyzing motion in two dimensions.
  • Trigonometry is used to calculate components.
  • Resultant vectors combine the effect of multiple vectors.
