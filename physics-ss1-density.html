Density and Relative density

Definition of Density:Density of a substance is defined as the mass of the substance per unit volume.It is denoted by p and it is a scaler quantity

Formula for density: Density=Mass/Volume (measured in kgm-3(kg/m³))

The S.I unit of density is gcm-3(g/cm³)

NB:

    (i) volume=cross sectional area×height
    (ii)Cross sectional area=Length×breadth
    (iii)volume=Length× breadth
    Thus:m=pv=pAh

Definition of Relative density:Relative density is the density of a water compared with the density of other substance

Formula for Relative density:

  • Relative density=Density of substance/Density of water
  • Relative density=mass of substance/mass of equal volume of water
  • Relative density=Weight of substances/weight of equal volume of substance

    Example: Determine the mass of an aluminum whose density is 2700kg/m³, if the volume of the aluminum is 0.08 m³

    Solution

  • mass=?
  • volume=0.08m³
  • Density=2700kg/m³
      • Density=mass/volume
      2700=mass/0.08

      mass=0.08×2700=216kg

    Example II: what is the height of a cylinderical iron if the density is 7900kg/m³? The mass is 700kg and the radius is 0.1m(π=22/7)
