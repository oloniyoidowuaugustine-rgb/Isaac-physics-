Topic: Projectile Motion

1. Definition

Projectile motion is the motion of an object thrown into the air, moving under the **influence of gravity**, along a curved path called a parabola.

2. Components of Motion

  • Horizontal motion: Uniform motion, constant velocity (no horizontal acceleration)
  • Vertical motion: Accelerated motion, acceleration = g (gravity)

3. Key Formulas

  • Horizontal displacement: x = u cosθ × t
  • Vertical displacement: y = u sinθ × t - ½ g t²
  • Time of flight: T = (2 u sinθ) / g
  • Maximum height: H = (u² sin²θ) / (2 g)
  • Horizontal range: R = (u² sin 2θ) / g

4. Notes

  • u = initial velocity, θ = angle of projection
  • g = acceleration due to gravity (~9.8 m/s²)
  • Motion is 2-dimensional, combining horizontal and vertical components
  • Horizontal and vertical motions are independent

5. Example

Example: A ball is thrown with u = 20 m/s at θ = 30° Time of flight: T = 2 × 20 × sin30° / 9.8 ≈ 2.04 s Maximum height: H = (20² × sin²30°) / (2 × 9.8) ≈ 10.2 m Horizontal range: R = (20² × sin60°) / 9.8 ≈ 35.3 m
