Topics: Molecular Force & Elastic Property of Solids

1. Molecular Force

Molecular force is the force of attraction or repulsion that acts between molecules of a substance.

Types:

  • Cohesive forces: Attraction between molecules of the same substance
  • Adhesive forces: Attraction between molecules of different substances

Importance:

  • Determines surface tension
  • Determines capillarity
  • Affects boiling and melting points

2. Elastic Property of Solids

Elasticity is the property of a solid to regain its original shape and size after removing the deforming force.

Hooke’s Law: The extension of a spring is directly proportional to the applied force, provided the elastic limit is not exceeded.

F = k × x
Where:
F = force, k = spring constant, x = extension
Example: A spring stretches 0.1 m when a force of 20 N is applied. Find the spring constant.
k = F / x = 20 / 0.1 = 200 N/m
