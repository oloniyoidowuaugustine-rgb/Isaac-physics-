Topic: Application of SHM

1. Definition

Applications of Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) are found in many practical devices where periodic motion is used.

2. Common Applications

  • Clocks: Pendulum clocks use SHM for accurate time measurement
  • Seismographs: Detect earthquakes through oscillations
  • Musical instruments: Vibrating strings and air columns
  • Vehicle suspension systems: Shock absorbers rely on spring oscillations
  • Engineering: Bridges and buildings tested for vibrations

3. Notes

  • SHM ensures energy-efficient oscillations in devices.
  • Amplitude, period, and frequency are critical for design.
  • Understanding SHM helps in controlling vibrations in structures.
Example: A pendulum in a clock swings back and forth in SHM. Its period is adjusted by changing the length of the pendulum for accurate timekeeping.
