Topic: Electric Field

1. Definition

An electric field is a region around a charged body where another charge experiences a force.

2. Electric Field Intensity

E = F / q

Where:
E = electric field strength (N/C)
F = force experienced (N)
q = charge (C)

3. Electric Field due to a Point Charge

E = kQ / r²

k = 9 × 10⁹ Nm²/C²
Q = source charge
r = distance from charge

4. Electric Field Lines

  • Field lines originate from positive charges
  • Field lines terminate at negative charges
  • Closer lines indicate stronger field
  • Field lines never cross

5. Example

Example: A force of 0.2 N acts on a charge of 0.01 C. E = F/q = 0.2 / 0.01 = 20 N/C

6. Notes

  • Electric field is a vector quantity.
  • Direction is the direction of force on a positive test charge.
  • Electric fields exist even in vacuum.
