Speed Velocity & Acceleration

Definition of Speed:Speed is defined as the rate of change of distance moved in an unspecific direction. It is a scalar quantity

Formula for Speed:Speed=Distance moved/Time

Average speed: is the ratio of the total distance travelled to the total time taken

average speed=Total distance travelled/Total time taken

Definition of Velocity: Velocity is the rate of change of distance moved in a specific directions

Velocity=Displacement/Time

similarities between speed and velocity

  • Both are used in the measurement of distance
  • Both are measured in m/s or ms–¹

    • Example: A car travelled a distance of 4km in 40sec. Find the speed in metre per seconds

    solution

    The km is converted to m

    1000m=1km

    Thus:4×1000=4000m

    speed=Distance/Time

    speed=4000/40 =100m/s

    Linear Acceleration: linear acceleration is the rate of increase of velocity with time.If the velocity decrease with time it is called retardation

    Uniform acceleration: A body is said to undergo uniform acceleration, if the increasing rate of the change of velocity is constant,no matter how small the time interval may be

    Equation of motion

  • v²=u²+2as
