friction

When a body slide over the surface of another body, there is an opposing force acting opposite to the direction of motion.The opposite force is called frictionwhen a body is rolled on a rough surface, it will come to an end within a short period of time, die to the friction on the rough surface/strong>

Limiting force:when a body is placed over another, a force act between the surface in contact and this force is called limiting force

Application of friction

  • The tyres of a motor car and a bicycle are made rough to increase friction
  • It is the friction between the belt and the pulley that help in the rotation of the various parts of the machine
  • When the ground becomes slippery after rain, it is made rough by spreading sand in order not to caus slipping
  • Without friction, the brake of a motor car cannot work
  • Without friction between the feet and the ground,it will not be possible to walk without falling

    • Method of reducing friction

  • Streamling:Streamling is used to reduce friction due to the shape on an object which ensure that only a small surface area of the object is in head-on contact with the fluid during motion
  • Ball bearing:Shadt are fixed on ball bearing so that friction will be reduced because rolling friction is less than sliding friction.
  • Lubricating: Lubricants are viscous liquid or oil which are applied between two surface in contact. They help in overcoming or reducing friction
  • Polishing: Friction between two surface in contact can be reduced by polishing them
  • Banking:This is the method used in reducing sideways friction

    • Disadvantage of Friction

  • It reduce the efficiency of machine
  • It cause the heating of engines
  • It reduces motion
  • It causes wear and tear in machine
