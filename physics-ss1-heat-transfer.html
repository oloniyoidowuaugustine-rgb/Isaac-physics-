Mode of heat transfer

Heat can be transferred from one place to another in three media. These are solid, liquid and gas.process of heat transfer in each medium are conduction, convention and radiation

Conduction

Conduction is the transfer of heat through material from one place to another, by the vibration or motion of the molecules of the material

Insulator and Conductor s

Insulators are material that do not all electric charge to pass through them example papers, stone etc.

    Conductors are material that allows electric charge to pass through them examples copper, gold, iron etc.

Convention

Convention is the transfer of fluid e.g gas and liquid. It is the process by which heat energy is transferred in fluid by the actual movement of the heated fluid

Radiation

Radiation is the transfer of heat from one point to another without heating the intervening medium. Radiation gives off a form of energy which is called radient energy.
