Concept of position and motion

concept of position

Position of a point is the distance between two perpendicular lines.The lines are called x-axis and y-axis and their interception is called origin.

concept of motion

Motion can be defined as the change of a position of a body with time.

Type of motion

  • Translational Rectilinear motion:This is a type of motion which occur when an object move in a fixed direction.
  • Random motion:This is a type of motion in which a body moves in a non linear manner.
  • Rotational motion:This is a movement of a body in a rotational manner about its axis.
  • oscillatory motion:This is the to and fro movement of a body about a fixed point.
  • Relative motion:This is the movement of an object in relation to another object

    • Force causes motion

    Type of force

  • contact force:This exist when there is a touch, hold or a contact with the object in question
  • Field force:This is confirmed in a given region or space
