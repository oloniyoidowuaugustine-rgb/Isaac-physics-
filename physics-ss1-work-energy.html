Work, Energy and Power

Work

Definition of work:Work is defined as the product of force and perpendicular distance in the direction of the applied force.It is denoted by w, measured in joules (j) and a scaler quantity.

    Thus, work =force×distance
    W=F×S
    since f=ma, W=F×S=ma×s

Example:A constant force of 40N acting on a body initially at rest give it an acceleration of 0.1m/s² for 4sec. calculate the work done by the force.

    solution
    U=0, a=0.1m/s², t=4s, F=40N
    S=ut+½at²(from motion equation)
    0×t+½×0.1×4²
    S=0.8m
    w=f×s
    w=40×0.8
    w=32j

Energy

Definition of energy:Energy is the ability or capacity to do work. It is denoted by E, measured in joules (j) and a scaler quantity

Forms of Energy

  • mechanical energy
  • electrical energy
  • solar or light energy
  • nuclear/atomic energy
  • sound energy
  • chemical energy
  • heat energy

    • Conservation of energy:The principle of conservation of energy state that in a closed system, energy can neither be created nor destroyed during a transformation, though energy can be transformed so that the total energy of a given system remain constant

    Mechanical Energy

    Mechanical energy is a form of energy which is generally used in our daily activities by individual and object. There are two types of mechanical energy:

  • kinetic energy (KE):It is defined as the energy due to the motion. it is denoted by K.E, measured in joules and it's a scaler quantity

    • Example:A body of mass 4kg is acted upon by a constant force of 12N for 2sec. calculate the K.E gained by the body

      solution
      u=0, f=12N, m=4kg, t=2sec
      K.E=½mv²(we have to find fo v)
      F=ma
      a=12/4 =3m/s
      v=u+at
      v=0+3×2=6
      K.E=½×4×6²=72j
  • Potential Energy: potential energy is defined as the energy at rest due to its level or position above the earth surface, it is denoted by P.E, measured in joules and a scaler quantity

      P.E=mgh

    • Example: A fruit from a tree of mass 2kg is 5m above the ground. calculate the P.E of the fruit above the ground {g=10m/s}

      Solution
      P.E=mgh
      2×10×5=100j

    Power

    power is the rate of work done or the rate at which energy is expanded.It is denoted by P, measured in watts (w)-S.I units, J/s or Nm/s

  • P=F×S/t
  • P=F×S/t
  • Thus, 1 watt (1 j/s) is defined as the unit of work done by one object (1 j) in one second
