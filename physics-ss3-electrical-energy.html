Topic: Electrical Energy, Power & Cost

1. Electrical Energy

Electrical energy is the work done when electric charge flows through a potential difference.

E = V × I × t

E = energy (Joules)
V = voltage (Volts)
I = current (Amperes)
t = time (seconds)

2. Electrical Power

Power is the rate at which electrical energy is consumed.

P = V × I
P = I² × R
P = V² / R

P = power (Watts)
R = resistance (Ohms)

3. Cost of Electrical Energy

Electric companies charge for energy consumed, usually in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

Cost = Energy consumed (kWh) × Unit cost (₦/kWh)

4. Examples

Example 1: A 60W bulb is used for 5 hours. Energy consumed = P × t = 60 × 5 = 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh If 1 kWh costs ₦50, cost = 0.3 × 50 = ₦15
Example 2: A 100Ω resistor carries 2A current. P = I² × R = 2² × 100 = 400 W

5. Notes

  • 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • Electrical energy depends on voltage, current, and time.
  • Always convert hours to seconds if calculating in joules.
