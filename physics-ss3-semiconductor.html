Topic: Semiconductor Electronics

1. Definition

A semiconductor is a material whose conductivity lies between a conductor and an insulator. Conductivity can be controlled by adding impurities (doping).

2. Types of Semiconductors

  • Intrinsic: Pure semiconductor (e.g., pure silicon, germanium).
  • Extrinsic: Doped semiconductor to increase conductivity.

3. Doping

  • N-type: Doping with pentavalent atom → extra electrons (negative charge carriers).
  • P-type: Doping with trivalent atom → creates holes (positive charge carriers).

4. PN Junction

Formed when P-type and N-type semiconductors are joined. Acts as a diode allowing current to flow in one direction.

5. Applications

  • Diodes and LEDs
  • Transistors (NPN & PNP)
  • Solar cells
  • Integrated circuits (ICs)

6. Notes

  • Semiconductors are the foundation of modern electronics.
  • PN junctions control the flow of current.
  • Doping changes conductivity and type of charge carriers.
