Topic: Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies

1. Definition

A rigid body is in equilibrium when it is **stationary or moving uniformly** and the **net force and net torque on it are zero**.

2. Conditions for Equilibrium of Rigid Body

  • ΣF = 0 → The sum of all forces acting on the body = 0
  • Στ = 0 → The sum of all moments (torques) about any point = 0

3. Moment of a Force (Torque)

Torque (τ) is the turning effect of a force about a point:

τ = F × d

Where:
F = force applied
d = perpendicular distance from pivot to line of action of force

4. Types of Equilibrium

  • Stable equilibrium: Returns to original position after small displacement
  • Unstable equilibrium: Moves further away after small displacement
  • Neutral equilibrium: Remains in new position after displacement

5. Example

Example: A uniform beam of 10 N is supported at its center. If a 5 N weight is placed at one end, a force of 5 N at the other end keeps the beam balanced. Στ = 5×L/2 - 5×L/2 = 0 → Beam is in equilibrium

6. Notes

  • Both force and torque must be considered for rigid body equilibrium.
  • Applications include bridges, levers, and mechanical structures.
  • Choose pivot points carefully when calculating moments.
