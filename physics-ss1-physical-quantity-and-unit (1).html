physical quantity and unit

Physical quantity is any property that can be measured

Type of physical quantity

  • Fundamental unit:

    Fundamental quantity are the accepted standard quantity or measures in which measurement is made.

    Example of fundamental unit are

      length(metre)
      mass(kg)
      Time(s)
      current(ampere)
      Temperature(kelvin)
      luminous intensity(candela)
      magnetic flux(weber)

    Derived unit

    Derived quantity are quantity obtained from fundamental quantity in form of combination.

    Example of derived unit are

      Acceleration(m/s²)
      velocity(m/s)

    Dimensions of physical quantity

    Dimensions of physical quantity simply means the way it is relate to fundamental quantity.

    Example

      speed=Distance/time =L/T
