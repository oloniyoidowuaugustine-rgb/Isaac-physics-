Topic: Wave Motion – Fundamentals

1. Definition

A wave is a **disturbance that travels through a medium**, transferring energy without transferring matter.

2. Types of Waves

  • Mechanical Waves: Require a medium (sound, water waves)
  • Electromagnetic Waves: Do not require a medium (light, radio waves)
  • Transverse Waves: Particles vibrate perpendicular to wave direction
  • Longitudinal Waves: Particles vibrate parallel to wave direction

3. Wave Parameters

  • Wavelength (λ): distance between two consecutive crests or troughs
  • Frequency (f): number of oscillations per second
  • Period (T): time for one complete oscillation
  • Wave velocity (v): speed at which the wave travels
v = f × λ

4. Example

Example: A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Velocity: v = f × λ = 50 × 2 = 100 m/s

5. Notes

  • Energy is transferred in the direction of wave propagation.
  • Amplitude determines wave energy; larger amplitude → more energy.
  • Mechanical waves cannot travel in vacuum.
