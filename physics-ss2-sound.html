Topic: Wave Equation & Physical Wave Properties

1. Wave Equation

The wave equation relates **wave speed (v), frequency (f), and wavelength (λ)**:

v = f × λ

2. Physical Wave Properties

  • Amplitude (A): Maximum displacement of particles
  • Wavelength (λ): Distance between consecutive crests or troughs
  • Frequency (f): Number of waves per second
  • Period (T): Time for one complete oscillation: T = 1/f
  • Wave speed (v): Speed at which wave propagates
  • Phase: Position of a point in wave cycle

3. Example

Example: A wave has wavelength 0.5 m and frequency 200 Hz. Speed: v = f × λ = 200 × 0.5 = 100 m/s

4. Notes

  • Amplitude determines wave energy; higher amplitude → more energy
  • Wave speed depends on medium properties (density, tension, elasticity)
  • Wave can be transverse or longitudinal, affecting particle motion direction
