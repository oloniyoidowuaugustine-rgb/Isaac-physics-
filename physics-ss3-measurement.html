Topic: Electric Measurements

1. Ammeter

An ammeter measures electric current.

  • Connected in series
  • Has very low resistance

2. Voltmeter

A voltmeter measures potential difference.

  • Connected in parallel
  • Has very high resistance

3. Galvanometer

A galvanometer detects small currents.

4. Converting Galvanometer

To Ammeter:

Add a shunt resistor in parallel.

To Voltmeter:

Add a high resistance in series.

5. Wheatstone Bridge

P/Q = R/S

Used to measure unknown resistance accurately.

6. Potentiometer

Used to measure e.m.f without drawing current.

E₁ / E₂ = L₁ / L₂

7. Example

Example: If P = 2Ω, Q = 4Ω, R = 6Ω, find S. P/Q = R/S 2/4 = 6/S 1/2 = 6/S S = 12Ω

8. Notes

  • Ammeter → series connection
  • Voltmeter → parallel connection
  • Wheatstone bridge gives precise resistance values
