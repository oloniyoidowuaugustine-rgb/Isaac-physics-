Topic: Magnetism & Magnetic Field

1. Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where magnetic forces can be felt.

2. Magnetic Field Lines

  • They emerge from the north pole and enter the south pole.
  • They never cross.
  • They are closer where the field is stronger.

3. Magnetic Force on a Current-Carrying Conductor

F = B × I × L × sinθ

F = force (N)
B = magnetic flux density (T)
I = current (A)
L = length of conductor in field (m)
θ = angle between conductor and magnetic field

4. Right-Hand Rule

Thumb → direction of current Fingers → direction of magnetic field Palm → direction of force on conductor

5. Magnetic Effect of a Current

  • A current produces a magnetic field around it (Ampère’s law).
  • Used in electromagnets, motors, and relays.

6. Example

A conductor 0.5 m long carries 2A at right angles to a magnetic field of 0.3 T. F = BIL sinθ = 0.3 × 2 × 0.5 × sin90° = 0.3 N

7. Notes

  • Magnetic field is measured in Tesla (T).
  • Magnetic effect of current is the principle behind electric motors.
  • Direction of force is given by Fleming’s left-hand rule for motors.
