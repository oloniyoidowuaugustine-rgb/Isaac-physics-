Circular motion

Circular motion is a motion on a circular path in which the speed of the body remais constant while it's direction continuously changes e.g …

  • stone whirled in horizontal circle
  • Cloth ending in a spin drier
  • The earth moving round the sun
  • planet going round the sun

    • Angular velocity

    Angular velocity is defined as the change of the angle per seconds, measure in rad/s

    Formula's for angular velocity:

  • ∅=wt
  • s=r∅
  • v=rw
  • T=2π/w
  • 1/f

    • Centripetal force:When a stone is attached to a string and whirled round at constant speed in a horizontal circle, the stone moves with constant (same) speed but at different direction and acceleration directed towards the center of the circle due to tension in the string that produce a force

    The force required to keep thie stone moving in this circular path is called centripetal force

    Centripetal force=mv²/r OR mrw²
