Topic: Image Formation with Lenses

1. Definition

Lenses are **transparent optical devices** that refract light to form images. Two types of lenses:

  • Convex (converging) lenses
  • Concave (diverging) lenses

2. Lens Formula

1/f = 1/v - 1/u

Where:
f = focal length
u = object distance
v = image distance

3. Magnification

m = h'/h = v/u

h = object height, h' = image height

4. Ray Diagrams

  • Convex lens: parallel ray → passes through focus; ray through center → straight line
  • Concave lens: parallel ray → diverges as if from focus; ray through center → straight line
  • Images can be real or virtual depending on object position

5. Examples

Example: An object is placed 15 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 10 cm. Using lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u → 1/10 = 1/v - 1/(-15) → 1/v = 1/10 + 1/15 = 5/30 → v = 6 cm Image is real and inverted

6. Notes

  • Focal length is positive for convex, negative for concave lenses.
  • Magnification > 1 → image larger; < 1 → image smaller.
  • Lens combination can form complex optical instruments (microscope, telescope).
