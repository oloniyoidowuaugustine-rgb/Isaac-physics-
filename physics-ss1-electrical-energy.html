Topic: Electrical Energy and Electrical Power

1. Electrical Energy

Electrical energy is the energy supplied by an electric current. It is the work done when electric charges move through a circuit.

Electrical Energy (E) = Voltage (V) × Current (I) × Time (t)

E = VIt

Unit of electrical energy = Joule (J)

Other Formula for Electrical Energy

Since V = IR

E = I²Rt

OR

E = (V² / R) t

2. Commercial Unit of Electrical Energy

In homes, electrical energy is not measured in joules. It is measured in kilowatt-hour (kWh).

1 kilowatt-hour (1 kWh) = 1000 watts used for 1 hour
1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ Joules

3. Electrical Power

Electrical power is the rate at which electrical energy is used. It is how fast energy is consumed.

Power (P) = Voltage × Current

P = VI

Unit of power = Watt (W)

Other Forms of Power Formula

Since V = IR

P = I²R

OR

P = V² / R

4. Relationship Between Power and Energy

Electrical Energy (E) = Power (P) × Time (t)

E = Pt

5. Worked Examples

Example 1: A bulb operates at 220V and draws 2A.
Find the power.

P = VI
P = 220 × 2
P = 440W
Example 2: A 100W bulb is used for 5 hours.
Find the energy consumed in kWh.

Energy = Power × Time
= 100W × 5 hours
= 500Wh
= 0.5 kWh
Example 3: Convert 1 kWh to Joules.

1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
