Electric charge and Electrostatic induction

Electrostatic Induction

Electrostatic induction is the process of charging a body by introducing a charged body to a neutral body. when this is done, the body is said to be induced with a charge

Gold-leaf Electroscope

Gold leaf electroscope is an instrument for measuring, indicating and detecting charges.

Capacitor

A capacitor is a device for storing electric charge.

Arrangements of a Capacitor

A capacitor is areanged in series or in a parallel arrangements

Capacitor in parallel arrangements

C=C1+C2+C3+............+Cn

    Hence, the capacitance of a capacitor connected in parallel is the sum of their individual capacitance

Capacitor in series arrangement

¹/C=¹/C1+¹/C2+¹/C3+......¹/Cn

    Hence, the capacitance of a capacitor connected in series is the sum of their reciprocal
