Topic: Electric Potential & Capacitance

1. Electric Potential

Electric potential at a point is the work done per unit charge in bringing a test charge from infinity to that point.

V = W / q

V = potential (Volts)
W = work done (Joules)
q = charge (Coulombs)

2. Potential Due to a Point Charge

V = kQ / r

k = 9 × 10⁹ Nm²/C²
Q = charge
r = distance

3. Potential Difference

Potential difference is the work done in moving a unit charge between two points.

V = IR

4. Capacitance

Capacitance is the ability of a capacitor to store electric charge.

C = Q / V

C = capacitance (Farads)
Q = charge stored
V = potential difference

5. Energy Stored in a Capacitor

E = ½ C V²

6. Example

Example: A capacitor stores 0.02 C at 10 V. C = Q/V = 0.02 / 10 = 0.002 F

7. Notes

  • Unit of potential is Volt (V).
  • Unit of capacitance is Farad (F).
  • Capacitors are used in radios, TVs, and power supplies.
