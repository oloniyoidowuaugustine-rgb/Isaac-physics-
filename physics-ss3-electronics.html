Topic: Electronics (Diodes & Transistors)

1. Diode

A diode is a semiconductor device that allows current to flow in one direction only.

  • Forward biased → current flows.
  • Reverse biased → current does not flow.
  • Used for rectification (AC → DC conversion).

2. Light Emitting Diode (LED)

  • Emits light when current flows through it.
  • Used in displays, indicators, and lighting.

3. Transistor

A transistor is a semiconductor device used as an amplifier or a switch.

  • Has three terminals: Base (B), Collector (C), Emitter (E).
  • Current at the base controls larger current from collector to emitter.
  • Used in amplification and switching circuits.

4. Types of Transistors

  • NPN
  • PNP

5. Applications

  • Amplifiers
  • Switches in digital circuits
  • Oscillators
  • Radio and TV circuits

6. Example

Example: A diode in forward bias with 0.7V across it allows current to flow. In reverse bias, the same diode blocks the current.

7. Notes

  • Diodes are the building blocks of rectifiers.
  • Transistors can amplify small signals into larger ones.
  • Electronics is based on semiconductors.
