Topic: Latent Heat

1. Definition

Latent heat is the amount of heat energy required to **change the state of a substance** without changing its temperature.

2. Types of Latent Heat

  • Latent Heat of Fusion (Lf): Heat required to change 1 kg of a solid to liquid at melting point.
  • Latent Heat of Vaporization (Lv): Heat required to change 1 kg of a liquid to gas at boiling point.

3. Formula

Heat absorbed or released:

Q = m × L

Where:
Q = Heat energy (Joules)
m = Mass of the substance (kg)
L = Latent heat (J/kg) (Lf or Lv)

4. Examples

Example 1: 2 kg of ice melts at 0°C. Latent heat of fusion of ice = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg. Q = m × L = 2 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 6.72 × 10⁵ J
Example 2: 1.5 kg of water boils at 100°C. Latent heat of vaporization = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg. Q = 1.5 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 3.39 × 10⁶ J

5. Notes

  • During phase change, temperature remains constant.
  • Latent heat explains why ice melts and water boils without changing temperature initially.
