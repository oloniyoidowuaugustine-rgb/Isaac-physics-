Heat and Temperature

Heat

Definition of Heat:Heat is the total internal energy of a body which can be transferred from one place to another.Heat depends on temperature difference. Heat is measured in joules and it is named after Prescott joules (1819-1889)

Temperature

Meaning of temperature: Temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness of a body

Measurement of Temperature

The unit of measurement of temperature are fahrenhei, Celcius and Ranke named after Gabriel Daniel Fahrenheit, Anders Celcius and Augustus Ranke of Poland respectively. Temperature is measured using Thermometer. The first thermometer was constucted by celcius and it was called centigrade scale. The scale has two fixed point, the upper fixed point and the lower fixed point.

A thermometer must possess this following qualities

  • Construction: A thermometer must posses certain properties before it is used. In a mecury thermometer, the thermometer must contain a mecury which expand as a result of increase in temperature. The mecury in the thermometer is called
    • thermometric substance
  • Calibration:A thermometer must be well calibrated, starting with two reference point.The calibration must be made with a shiny ink that can be viewed
  • Sensitivity:A thermometer is said to be sensitive if (i) it can detect small change in temperature (ii)it show the temperature of a body in a short time(iii)it does not absorb large amount of heat for its own heating or cooling from the body whose temperature is measured
