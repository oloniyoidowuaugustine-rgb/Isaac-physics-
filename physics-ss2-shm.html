Topic: Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

1. Definition

Simple Harmonic Motion is a type of periodic motion where the **restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement** and acts towards the equilibrium position.

2. Displacement, Velocity & Acceleration

  • Displacement: x = A cos(ωt + φ)
  • Velocity: v = -ω A sin(ωt + φ)
  • Acceleration: a = -ω² x

Where:
A = amplitude, ω = angular frequency, φ = phase constant, t = time

3. Period and Frequency

  • Period: T = 2π / ω (time for one complete oscillation)
  • Frequency: f = 1 / T (number of oscillations per second)

4. Examples of SHM

  • Simple pendulum
  • Mass-spring system
  • Vibrating tuning fork

5. Notes

  • Restoring force is always directed towards equilibrium.
  • Motion is sinusoidal in nature.
  • Energy oscillates between kinetic and potential forms.
Example: A mass-spring system with amplitude 0.2 m and angular frequency 5 rad/s. Maximum velocity: Vmax = ωA = 5 × 0.2 = 1 m/s Maximum acceleration: Amax = ω²A = 25 × 0.2 = 5 m/s²
