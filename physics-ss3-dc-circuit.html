Topic: Current Electricity (Advanced Circuit Analysis)

1. Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge.

I = Q / t

I = current (Ampere)
Q = charge (Coulomb)
t = time (seconds)

2. Ohm’s Law

V = IR

V = voltage (Volts)
I = current (Ampere)
R = resistance (Ohms)

3. Electrical Power

P = VI
P = I²R
P = V² / R

4. Resistors in Series

R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...
  • Same current flows through each resistor
  • Total resistance increases

5. Resistors in Parallel

1/R_total = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...
  • Same voltage across each resistor
  • Total resistance decreases

6. Kirchhoff’s Laws

First Law (Current Law)

The total current entering a junction equals the total current leaving.

Second Law (Voltage Law)

The sum of voltage gains equals the sum of voltage drops in a closed loop.

7. Example

Example: Two resistors 4Ω and 6Ω in series. R_total = 4 + 6 = 10Ω If connected to 20V source: I = V/R = 20 / 10 = 2A

8. Notes

  • Unit of current is Ampere (A).
  • Unit of resistance is Ohm (Ω).
  • Kirchhoff’s laws are used for complex circuit analysis.
